# Why LeetFaith

*Two years ago, I came across Matthew 13:37 and immediately thought of 1337, something I loved from my early gaming days. That eventually led to LeetFaith, and while I'm still not sure what it'll become, I figured I'd start somewhere.*

*October 31, 2026*

About two years ago, I had an idea come to mind while studying the book of Matthew. I was reading Matthew 13:37 when something caught my attention: **1337**, otherwise known as *leet*.

For anyone who grew up gaming in the early 2000s, you'll probably understand why that caught my attention. Back then, 1337 was one of the "coolest" things ever. I loved randomly coming across that number in the wild, whether it was a damage number in World of Warcraft or somewhere completely unexpected.

Fast-forward to my career as a software engineer, where I was also familiar with LeetCode. Somewhere between LeetCode, 1337, and my faith, the name **LeetFaith** came to mind.

I knew I wanted to do something with it. I just wasn't sure what.

So, two years ago, I registered the domain, spun up a super simple app that displayed Matthew 13:37, and hosted it on Vercel.

That initial concept is preserved in the [first LeetFaith commit](https://github.com/jucooper/leetfaith/commit/1f5e2f2a9ef3ea52264a9007f869765a25985d20), dated June 7, 2024.

And that was pretty much it.

I've had some big aspirations for LeetFaith over the years, but I've also come to realize that I don't need to force it into becoming something. If God calls me to do something more with it, I'll pursue that. Until then, I'm content to patiently wait and see where He leads.

I was reminded of something similar when I was listening to Philip Anthony Mitchell's sermon on Acts 1 & 2. TLDR; He explained that a season of preparation may last days, months, or years.

For now, LeetFaith will just be a place to jot down my thoughts. Thoughts on the intersection of software engineering and faith, things I'm learning through my Bible studies, and whatever else comes to mind along the way.
